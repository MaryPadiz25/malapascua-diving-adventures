MALAPASCUA DIVE SITES

World-Class Dive Sites Around Malapascua Island

Malapascua Island is globally recognized as one of the top scuba diving destinations in the Philippines. From the legendary thresher shark cleaning station at Kimud Shoal to the rich biodiversity of Gato Island Marine Reserve, every dive is an unforgettable experience.

🦈

Kimud Shoal

Thresher Shark Dive Site — 15–30m Depth

The world's premier site for thresher shark encounters. Divers descend at dawn to witness pelagic thresher sharks at their cleaning station. Occasional manta ray sightings too. Advanced Open Water required.

⚓

Gato Island

Marine Reserve Tunnel Dive — 8–22m

A protected marine reserve with a stunning underwater tunnel full of soft corals, white-tip reef sharks, sea snakes, and macro life. One of the most unique dives in Cebu. All certification levels welcome.

📷

Lighthouse Reef

Mandarin Fish Night Dive — 5–12m

Famous for its nightly mandarin fish mating display — a kaleidoscopic spectacle you won't forget. Shallow depth makes it ideal for beginners and macro underwater photographers.

🚢

Doña Marilyn Wreck

Historical Wreck Dive — 18–32m

A passenger ferry that sank in a 1988 typhoon, now encrusted with corals and inhabited by lionfish, scorpionfish, and glassfish. An atmospheric deep dive for advanced divers.

🌊

Chocolate Island

Macro Paradise — Max 18m

A macro diver's dream with frogfish, pipefish, seahorses, and nudibranchs on a rich sandy slope. Excellent for underwater photography. Great for Open Water divers and up.

🐋

Monad Shoal

Big Pelagic Encounters — 20–40m

One of the few dive sites where you stand a real chance of encountering tiger sharks, whale sharks, manta rays, or hammerheads on a single dive. For experienced divers only.

🗓️ Best time for thresher sharks: November–May · Early morning dives (5:30–6 AM departure)

Everything You Need to Know About Malapascua Diving

Malapascua Island is one of the only places in the world where you can reliably dive with pelagic thresher sharks at their cleaning station (Kimud Shoal). Beyond threshers, you'll find the Gato Island marine reserve tunnel, the historic Doña Marilyn wreck, mandarin fish at Lighthouse Reef, macro paradise at Chocolate Island, and big pelagic encounters at Monad Shoal — all within a short boat ride from the island.

November through May is peak diving season with outstanding visibility of 20–30m, calm seas, and the most frequent thresher shark sightings. December to February offers the most stable, sunny weather. June to October brings rougher seas but far fewer tourists — and excellent macro photography conditions are available year-round.

Thresher shark dives at Kimud Shoal require a minimum SSI or PADI Advanced Open Water certification due to the 18–30m depth. For most other dive sites around Malapascua (Gato Island, Lighthouse Reef, Chocolate Island), an Open Water certification is sufficient. Complete beginners can start with our Discover Scuba Diving (DSD) program — no certification required.

GETTING TO MALAPASCUA

How to Get to Malapascua Island from Cebu

Malapascua Island is about 4–5 hours north of Cebu City. Here are your two options — we can arrange everything for you, or you can go independently.

🚐

Option 1 — Private Transfer (Recommended)

Easiest · Most Comfortable · Door to Island

  1. 1
    Airport pickup

    We arrange a private car from Mactan-Cebu International Airport (CEB) directly to Maya Port — no bus terminals, no transfers, no stress.

  2. 2
    Maya Port → Malapascua Island

    From Maya Port we take you on a private boat directly to Malapascua Island. Journey takes about 30–45 minutes.

📲 Book Your Transfer
🚌

Option 2 — Public Transport

Budget-Friendly · Takes 4–6 Hours Total

  1. 1
    Airport → North Bus Terminal

    Take a taxi or grab from Mactan-Cebu International Airport to the North Bus Terminal in Cebu City. About 45–60 minutes depending on traffic.

  2. 2
    Cebu City → Maya Port (Ceres Bus)

    Board a Ceres Bus bound for Maya, Daanbantayan. Journey takes approximately 4–5 hours. Buses depart regularly from early morning.

  3. 3
    Maya Port → Malapascua Island (Ferry)

    From Maya Port, take a public outrigger boat (bangka) to Malapascua Island. Boats run regularly and the crossing takes about 30–45 minutes.

💡 Helpful Tips

  • Earliest bus from Cebu: Aim to catch the 5–6 AM bus from the North Bus Terminal to arrive at Maya before noon and get the morning ferry.
  • Thresher shark dives depart at 5:30 AM — plan to arrive on the island the day before your dive.
  • Last ferry from Maya Port is usually around 5–6 PM. Don't miss it or you'll need to stay in Maya overnight.
  • Private transfer tip: We can also arrange your return trip — a private boat from Malapascua to Maya Port, then a private car back to the airport. Just ask!
